Big AI models, on your own PC.

Pelica is a free chat app for Windows that runs large AI models on your computer, using your graphics card, memory and SSD together. Your chats stay on your PC.

Download for Windows Source on GitHub

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, a processor with AVX2 and a graphics card with Vulkan drivers.

Runs on your PC

Models run on your own hardware. There's no Pelica server, and nothing is sent anywhere unless you use a feature that needs the internet.

Models bigger than your GPU

Pelica treats graphics memory, RAM and your SSD as one, so models far bigger than your graphics card still run.

Works with your files

Pick a folder and Pelica can read, edit and run things in it, asking you first. Projects, memory and connectors are built in.

Sync with Google, if you like

Sign in with Google to keep your chats the same on every PC, through a private folder in your own Google Drive.
