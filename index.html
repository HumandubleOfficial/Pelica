How it works

One memory, three tiers.

The biggest open models are mixtures of experts: each word only wakes a handful of the model's thousands of experts. Pelica keeps the busy ones close to the GPU and streams the rest from your SSD, before they're needed.

Hot experts on the GPU Warm in RAM Cold on the SSD
GPU

Graphics memory: the essentials

The parts every word needs, plus the hottest experts, live on the GPU, where the work is fastest. Attention runs entirely there.

RAM

Memory: the warm core

The next-hottest experts wait in RAM. Pelica tracks how highly the model ranks every expert, so one that's almost picked is already warm.

SSD

Your drive: everything else

The rest streams from your SSD. Pelica guesses the next layer's experts ahead of time (right about 99% of the time for the top pick) and keeps the drive busy every millisecond.

Privacy

Yours, by design.

Pelica isn't a service. It's an app on your computer, and it's open source, so anyone can check what it does. Read the privacy policy.

  • The model runs on your PC. What you type never leaves it unless you use web search or a connector.
  • No account needed, no telemetry, no ads and no usage tracking.
  • Sync stays yours. Signing in with Google syncs through a hidden folder in your own Drive. Pelica can't see your other files.
  • You approve every action. Commands and tools ask first, and you choose what's always allowed.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Pelica really free?

Yes. Pelica is open source under the MIT license, with no paid tier, no subscription and no limits. The models you download are free to use under their own licenses.

Do I need a powerful computer?

A gaming PC from the last few years is plenty. Small models run on most graphics cards with Vulkan drivers. The giant ones want a 12–16 GB graphics card, 32 GB of RAM and a fast NVMe SSD, and they get faster the faster your SSD is.

How can a 164 GB model run on a 16 GB graphics card?

Models like DeepSeek V4 are mixtures of experts: each word only uses a few of their thousands of experts. Pelica keeps the most-used experts on the GPU and in RAM, and streams the rest from your SSD, guessing which ones come next before they're needed.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once a model is downloaded, chatting needs no internet at all. Web search, connectors, updates and Google sync are the only things that go online, and only when you use them.

Does Pelica see my chats?

No. There is no Pelica server. Your chats are saved on your PC, and if you turn on sync, in a hidden folder in your own Google Drive. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Which models can I run?

gpt-oss, Qwen3 and Qwen3.8, DeepSeek V4, GLM, OLMoE, Mixtral-style models and other mixture-of-experts GGUF files from Hugging Face. The model library shows which fit your PC.

What about Mac or Linux?

Pelica is Windows-only for now. The engine is written in Rust and talks to the GPU through Vulkan, so other systems are possible later.
